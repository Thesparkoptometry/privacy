Privacy Policy & SMS Messaging Terms

The Spark Optometry

Effective date: August 6, 2026

The Spark Optometry respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when you visit our website, contact our office, or communicate with us by telephone, email, or text message.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your name, telephone number, and email address;
  • Information you provide when contacting our office;
  • Appointment-related requests and customer service communications; and
  • Information needed to respond to questions about receipts, eyewear, contact lenses, insurance, or office services.

2. How We Use Information

We use personal information for legitimate business purposes, including to:

  • Respond to patient and customer questions;
  • Provide informational and customer support communications;
  • Address appointment-related requests, including cancellations or scheduling questions;
  • Communicate about receipts, eyewear, contact lenses, insurance questions, and other service matters; and
  • Maintain and improve our office operations and patient experience.

3. SMS Messaging Privacy

The Spark Optometry uses SMS only for informational and one-to-one customer care communications. Patients commonly begin the conversation by calling our published office number, hearing a voicemail invitation to text, and choosing to send the first text message.

We may use your phone number to respond to questions, discuss appointment-related requests, provide service updates, and assist with matters such as receipts, eyewear, contact lenses, insurance questions, and office information.

Mobile information sharing: No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS opt-in data and consent are not sold, rented, shared, or transferred to external organizations for marketing purposes.

SMS registration and consent information is used only for business purposes related to providing the communications and services requested by the user. We may disclose information to service providers only as reasonably necessary to deliver messaging services, operate our systems, comply with law, or protect our legal rights. Such disclosures do not include sharing mobile opt-in or consent data for third-party marketing.

4. SMS Opt-Out and Help

You may stop receiving SMS messages at any time by replying STOP or UNSUBSCRIBE. After you opt out, you will receive a confirmation and no further SMS messages will be sent unless you initiate a new conversation or provide new consent.

For assistance, reply HELP, call (408) 244-2831, or email info@thesparkoptometry.com.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to provide requested services, and to comply with legal, regulatory, accounting, or reporting requirements.

7. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to ask questions about our privacy practices or request that we update or correct information you previously provided, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements.
